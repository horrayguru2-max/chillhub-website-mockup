Monthly Sales Metrics:
  • Total Billed = Total issued invoices and their Credit/Debit Note adjustments
  • Total Unbilled = Sales Orders where invoices have not yet been issued
  • Total Sales = Total Billed + Total Unbilled
Monthly Financial Metrics:

An invoice is a legal document. Once saved as Issued, it becomes a formal financial record. Modifying it after it has been sent to a customer or reported to tax authorities may cause compliance issues. Changes must be made through proper adjustments — issuing a Credit Note or cancelling the invoice (within 72 hours of LHDN validation).
According to the LHDN e-Invoice guidelines (section 2.3.6), cancellations can only occur within 72 hours of validation. After this period, adjustments must be made through a Debit or Credit Note — not by modifying the original e-Invoice directly.
Purchase
Inventory-related items (stock): Use the Purchase module. Purchase Delivery updates quantity on hand; Purchase Invoice posts unit cost to the Inventory Account. When sold, cost is recognised under COGS using FIFO valuation.

Non-inventory items: Use the Expenses module. Record under Cost of Goods Sold or the relevant expense account.
Step 1 — Create Purchase Order (Procurement Team): Purchase Order Page → New → enter date and supplier → Items tab → add products with qty & price → View Document (send to supplier) → Save.

Step 2 — Record Stock Arrival (Warehouse Team): Open PO → Delivery tab → New → enter Delivery Note No. and date from supplier → enter Qty to Deliver → select warehouse in Auto Replenish To → Save.

Step 3 — Key in Purchase Invoice (Finance Team): Open PO → Invoice tab → New → enter supplier Invoice No. and date → enter quantity and unit price per item → Apply → Save.
  • Purchase Order (Procurement Team) — Confirms intent to buy. No stock or accounting entry yet. Calculates Open P/O Qty in Inventory.
  • Purchase Delivery (Warehouse Team) — Records actual goods received. Updates inventory quantity on hand for accurate stock levels.
  • Purchase Invoice (Finance Team) — Records supplier billing details and actual item cost. Posts accounting entries for correct valuation and reporting.
Supplier invoices may arrive after the goods. The separation ensures operational control (PO), inventory accuracy (Delivery), and financial correctness (Invoice) — each department manages its own responsibility.

A cost shortage occurs when purchase invoices have not yet been entered — the system cannot determine the item's actual cost. Once the purchase invoice is available, re-save the sales invoice to trigger recalculation. Alternatively: Invoice Page → More Actions → Update Cost Status to recalculate all Shortage-status invoices within the selected month.
"Calculated" means the system has determined the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) using perpetual inventory:
  • Upon receiving purchase invoices → item purchase value posted to Inventory Account
  • When sales invoices are issued → cost transferred from Inventory Account to COGS using FIFO (First-In, First-Out) valuation
Non-billable consumption deducts materials from inventory without charging the customer or showing the item on any invoice. It applies to Product items only — price cannot be set, but stock quantity on hand is still reduced on delivery. Use this to track internal usage of stock (e.g. materials used in-house).

The captured signature appears on the Signature line in the document.
