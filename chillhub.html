An invoice is a legal document. Once saved as Issued, it becomes a formal financial record. Modifying it after it has been sent to a customer or reported to tax authorities may cause compliance issues. Changes must be made through proper adjustments — issuing a Credit Note or cancelling the invoice (within 72 hours of LHDN validation).
According to LHDN e-Invoice guidelines, cancellations can only be made within 72 hours of validation. After this period, you must issue a Credit Note to reverse the original invoice, then create a new invoice with the corrected information.
Chillhub submits all consolidated e-Invoices individually, with automatic submission scheduled 7 days after the invoice is issued. This gives you a window to submit as an individual e-Invoice if the customer later requests one (e.g. for a B2B transaction).
Product: Specific goods, items, or parts tracked in inventory.
Product Type: Used when a specific product model is unknown or not tracked in the system.
Service: Additional charges or non-physical offerings your company provides.
Custom: Free-text entry for items not covered by predefined master data.
Total Billed = Total issued invoices and their Credit/Debit Note adjustments.
Total Unbilled = Sales Orders where invoices have not yet been issued.
Total Sales = Total Billed + Total Unbilled.
Total Revenue = Total Billed invoices + Credit & Debit Notes + Other Income within the selected month.

Note: Timing differences can occur. For example, a March invoice with an April Debit Note will appear in Total Billed (March), but the Debit Note posts to Total Revenue (April).

Qty On-hand: Actual physical stock in your warehouse.
Qty Available: Stock available for sale — On-hand minus reserved/allocated stock (where no delivery note has been created yet).
Pending Shipment: Allocated for outgoing shipments but not yet dispatched (delivery note created, shipping not done).
Pending Replenishment: Expected to arrive but not yet received (incoming delivery note created, replenishment not done).
Open P/O Qty: Quantity on open purchase orders, awaiting fulfillment from suppliers.
A cost shortage occurs when purchase invoices have not yet been entered, so the system cannot determine the item's actual cost. Once the purchase invoice is entered, re-save the sales invoice to trigger a cost recalculation. Alternatively, go to Invoice Page → More Actions → Update Cost Status to recalculate all shortage-status invoices for a selected month.
Check the Delivery Status of the invoice or sales order:

Status = "None": No delivery note has been created. Edit the invoice/sales order → Delivery tab → New → enter date and quantity → select warehouse in "Auto ship from" → Save.

Status = "Ready": Delivery note exists but items haven't been shipped yet. Edit the invoice → Delivery tab → select warehouse in "Auto ship from" → Save.
